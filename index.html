CookieBar

A cookie consent bar that appears only where privacy law calls for it, tells visitors which law applies where they are, and carries the Library's brand. Termly does the legal work underneath: location, blocking, consent records and Google Consent Mode.

Try it

This page uses a stand-in for Termly so the bar can be tried without a Termly account. "Start over" clears every choice. "Simulate a new visit" keeps your choice but starts a new session, which is when a visitor who declined is asked again.

What happens

  • Where the law calls for it, Termly shows the bar and holds back tracking until the visitor chooses. Everywhere else, the site works as usual.
  • Three small tabs keep the bar slim: Why this? shows the visitor's state or country, its outline and the law that applies there; What's collected and If you decline answer the next two questions.
  • Accept saves a first-party cookie, trpl_cookie_consent, with the date of acceptance. Termly keeps the official consent record.
  • Decline brings up a note that some features may be less personal or may not load.
  • A visitor who declined is asked again at the start of each new visit.

Install

trlibrary.com loads Termly and CookieBar through Google Tag Manager: Termly's template tag and a small CookieBar loader tag, both on the Consent Initialization trigger. The steps, the loader tag and the legal review list are in the CookieBar repository.
